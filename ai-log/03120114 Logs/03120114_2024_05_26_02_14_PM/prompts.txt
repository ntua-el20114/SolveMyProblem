This way, you can call `solve_vrp(data, max_distance=3000)` to solve a problem with a maximum distance constraint, or `solve_vrp(data, time_windows=my_time_windows)` to solve a problem with time window constraints, or `solve_vrp(data, max_distance=3000, time_windows=my_time_windows)` to solve a problem with both types of constraints.
